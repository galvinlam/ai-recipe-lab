    Language-aware by design.

    Cooking videos often mix languages, subtitles, slang, brand names, and casual quantities. This demo highlights how an AI workflow can preserve original phrasing while producing an English-friendly structured card for studying and cooking.

    繁體中文 「少許鹽、白胡椒，最後淋上香油」 becomes season lightly, finish with sesame oil.
    日本語 「弱火でじっくり、だしを入れて煮る」 becomes simmer gently over low heat with dashi.
    English “A splash,” “until glossy,” and “about a handful” become explicit notes with confidence flags.

    Confidence and gaps stay visible.

    Instead of pretending every video is perfectly structured, the interface surfaces uncertain quantities, missing cook times, and translation assumptions so a human can review the card.
